# Release 1.18.0 - Let the Aurora Open the Lane

Aurora Threshold carried the right colors but not yet the right rhythm. Its curtains arrived as thin joined fragments, its nebula light exposed concentric construction rings, and its authored hazards could recede into the same line language as the sky. This release opens the edges into continuous light, gives danger its own unmistakable voice, and leaves the center runway dark enough to read before committing.

## Draw the threshold as one current

- Replaces segmented aurora strokes with broad continuous procedural ribbons that keep their apparent weight during fractional parallax motion.
- Balances those curtains across both edges while protecting the center 45-55% lane from decorative dominance.
- Uses true Canvas radial gradients for Aurora Threshold's nebula light, removing conspicuous rings without changing other levels' procedural recipes.
- Centers wide background coverage as it drifts and prevents Level 1's dominant motif from repeating into the gameplay corridor.

## Give every hazard a distinct signal

- Renders ring-crossfire with a broad teal halo beneath a bright, narrow core.
- Renders debris-surge as straight steel cores with wider colored halos, separating those lane tells from the curved aurora behind them.
- Locks every canonical arc center, radius, angle, and debris endpoint in focused regression coverage.
- Keeps the existing resolved hazard intensity instead of attenuating it a second time inside the drawing pass.

## Inspect the fight at its true scale

- Stages the player, Scout, Fighter, Diver, asteroid, player projectile, and enemy projectile from their real procedural texture keys.
- Captures every subject at scale 1 with its authored logical and source-canvas dimensions recorded for review.
- Exercises ring-crossfire and debris-surge at 984x768 desktop and true 390x844 phone portrait.
- Samples the rendered center corridor against bright threat pixels so Lane-Reading remains an evidence-backed contract rather than a screenshot assumption.

## Keep encounter grammar fixed

- Leaves formations, spawn schedules, movement and projectile speeds, telegraph windows, hazard geometry, Arcade bodies, boss thresholds, damage rules, and pause behavior unchanged.
- Keeps Low, Standard, High, and Auto on their existing particle, layer-count, motif-density, atmosphere, and runtime-degradation budgets.
- Retains the fully procedural asset pipeline and introduces no new quality axis.

## Flight certification

- Passes both TypeScript compilers, formatting, ESLint, the complete isolated unit suite, level validation, Knip, architecture checks, dependency audit, production build, and bundle guardrails.
- Passes all 14 desktop/mobile visual scenarios, including authored-scale entity evidence and desktop/390px portrait hazard captures.
- Passes four applicable desktop/mobile performance scenarios with six intentional project skips and no threshold relaxation.
- Completes independent review of the backdrop, hazard hierarchy, and gameplay-scale evidence with no terminal findings.
