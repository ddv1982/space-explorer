# Release 1.20.1: Simpler internals, the same campaign

This maintenance release reduces repeated runtime wiring while preserving the ten-level campaign, controls, combat rules, upgrades, saves, music, and living procedural environments.

## Maintenance changes

- Gameplay frame behavior owns firing time and directly implements the frame contract.
- Enemy constructors, capacities, and contact behavior share one ordered roster.
- HUD widgets stay in one owned object. Announcement animation and resize continuation share one tween owner.
- Legacy skies remain available for development comparisons and are excluded from production. Texture preparation is synchronous; scene loading retains its asynchronous stale-request protection.
- Tests use official Bun types. Playwright reports actual test results and keeps screenshots for inspection instead of synthesizing visual passes.
- Architecture policies are checked against measured function identities, sizes, and complexity.

- Patched Vite and development-tool transitive dependencies to clear the high-severity audit gate.

## Publication gate

Prepared for publication after the maintenance PR is merged and its required checks and reviews pass. The full-stop workflow leaves the PR open and unmerged. Publish the release against the merged main revision, never the unmerged maintenance branch.

Screenshots and automated browser assertions do not measure human enjoyment or physical input latency. No gameplay tuning or performance improvement is claimed.
