# Release 1.20.0: Tighter flight and clearer combat

Short corrections now stop promptly, shots follow the upward centerline, and game over offers a direct fresh retry. This release focuses on flying, reading danger, and making another attempt through the existing ten-level campaign.

## Controls and first shots

- Movement uses normalized acceleration and a total speed cap, so diagonal travel matches cardinal speed. Hull banking remains visual.
- The player bullet texture and first physics object are prepared before firing, removing measured cold first-shot work from the update path.
- Brief Escape presses survive frame boundaries. Pause still freezes gameplay clocks.
- Development recordings include Enter, R, and M run actions while unrelated keys remain excluded.

## Hits, deaths, and retries

- Shield absorption has a blue ring and distinct audio. Hull damage retains a separate impact cue.
- Game over reports hostile fire, bombs, mines, beams, enemy contact, or debris as the fatal cause when known.
- Retry starts level 1 with fresh run resources while preserving checkpoints and settings. The result screen gives an existing score or progress target for the next attempt.
- Native Enter and Space make one upgrade purchase. Tab retains browser navigation, and scene shortcuts remain available.
- The final score includes rewards that complete after fatal contact. Duplicate death events cannot spend another reserve.

## Encounters and artwork

- Solar-flare warnings start visibly inside the viewport and leave a lower escape region. Lattice pressure remains.
- Hazard and ambush introductions are separated from their later combinations.
- Eight boss approaches provide time for deliberate midfield pickup interception. The campaign gains about 44 seconds across those approaches while earlier scripted waves keep their timestamps.
- Diver, Gunship, and Sower share a more consistent material treatment. Their silhouettes, logical dimensions, and collision geometry stay intact.
- The browser icon reuses the player artwork.

## Verification

The existing ten-level roster, upgrade system, damage-only difficulty settings, save format, and performance limits remain. The release includes local development recording and reproducible checks for controls, real collision outcomes, full beam lifetimes, retry state, and campaign initialization. Development diagnostics remain absent from production.

Automated coverage includes 124 unit-test files, both TypeScript compilers, lint, formatting, level validation, unused-code and architecture checks, build limits, and desktop, mobile, and portrait browser cases. The unchanged 5 ms update-cost gate also requires rejection of the 6 ms synthetic regression.

Human enjoyment, voluntary replay, and physical input-to-photon latency remain unmeasured. Staged encounter checks do not establish full-campaign balance.
