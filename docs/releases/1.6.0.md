# Release 1.6.0 — Kindle the Celestial Forge

The frontier burns with finer light. Ships cut sharper silhouettes through layered skies, command vessels carry living cores and shield halos, and every pilot can tune the forge to the strength of their machine without placing controls in the stars' address.

## A denser celestial forge

- Supersamples generated ships, enemies, projectiles, pickups, and particles while preserving their authored display size, origins, and Arcade Physics geometry.
- Separates premium skies into independently moving deep, motif, and atmosphere planes, restoring readable depth without returning to unbounded source-layer residency.
- Rebuilds all ten planet-arrival portraits at 1024×1024 with richer terrain, lighting, atmospheres, rings, structures, and campaign identity.
- Gives bosses render-only aura, shield, core, and hardpoint rigs that strengthen phase, shield, and Guard Break feedback without adding collision bodies.
- Expands bounded exhaust, impact, projectile, and boss effects while retaining the campaign's readable firing lanes.

## Quality belongs on the flight deck

- Adds Low, Standard, and High choices directly to the main menu and clearly marks the active tier.
- Persists the device-level choice in local storage and restores it on later visits.
- Uses Low for 1× entities and particles with two background planes, Standard for 2× entities and particles with all three planes, and High for 3× entities with 2× particles and all three planes.
- Reloads only after a changed tier is saved, ensuring generated textures and background layers never mix profiles in one session.
- Falls back safely to Standard when storage is missing, malformed, unsupported, or inaccessible.
- Removes the former player-facing quality query parameter while preserving development and browser-harness URLs.

## Steadier clocks and safer crossings

- Freezes wave choreography, hazard cadence, pooled enemy attack cycles, and boss Guard Break windows across physics-only pauses.
- Prevents stale preload font promises and timeout callbacks from completing a replacement scene lifecycle.
- Clamps persisted upgrades, score, health, shields, lives, and helper resources to configured campaign limits while preserving valid version-one saves.
- Deduplicates queued and in-flight planet portraits, keeps cached portraits available across resize restarts, and permits clean retry after loader cleanup.
- Performs lightweight resize layout immediately, cancels stale timers, and settles premium and level visuals exactly once.
- Separates the HULL label from its meter and keeps the health and Surge tracks aligned.

## Quality corridor

- Adds focused regression coverage for density-preserving generated textures, quality persistence and responsive selection, pause-safe gameplay clocks, preload ownership, save bounds, portrait loading, resize orchestration, and HUD geometry.
- Exercises the quality selector at desktop, phone portrait, and phone landscape sizes with persistence and reload checks.
- Passes TypeScript 7 and TypeScript 6 compatibility checks, lint, unit tests, level validation, Knip, production build, bundle budgets, desktop/mobile functional and visual browser gates, and isolated performance evidence.
