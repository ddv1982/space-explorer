# Release 1.17.0 - Let Every Silhouette Hold Its Line

The flight glass could name every threat, yet its smallest diagonals still betrayed the square lattice beneath the neon. Low power made those steps louder, and even the brightest setting asked a bilinear sampler to recover coverage it had never truly received. This cut tempers each procedural silhouette at high density, resolves it back into its authored frame, and returns the borrowed canvas before the next signal enters the lane.

## Temper the Cartographer's glass

- Draws procedural gameplay entities onto a temporary 4x generation surface before resolving them into their authored logical texture dimensions.
- Uses high-quality Canvas interpolation through the browser's premultiplied-alpha path, preventing dark fringe from gathering around neon edges.
- Keeps Phaser's linear runtime sampling for fractional motion after the source silhouette has already received a true coverage resolve.
- Removes the private frame-metadata repair that previously made high-density sources appear logical to sprites and Arcade bodies.

## Every threat keeps its authored mass

- Gives Low, Standard, High, and Auto the same 4x entity edge treatment so quality never weakens Lane-Reading.
- Leaves particle resolution, particle quantity, glow strength, motif density, background layers, and atmosphere on their existing tiered budgets.
- Preserves every texture key, logical frame, origin, display footprint, Arcade body, telegraph, projectile rule, boss threshold, and encounter schedule.
- Keeps the remake procedural; no offline ship atlas or new quality axis enters the hold.

## Return every borrowed lens

- Owns both the high-density drawing canvas and the logical resolve canvas until Phaser accepts the final texture.
- Returns unregistered logical canvases directly when texture construction fails.
- Removes registered textures by key when an add listener interrupts registration, allowing Phaser to release the canvas it owns.
- Preserves the original generation error even when best-effort cleanup also encounters trouble.

## Sentries watch the moving light

- Stages player and scout diagonals at two positions separated by exactly half a pixel.
- Captures Low, Standard, High, and Auto at desktop and 390px phone portrait, producing sixteen gameplay-canvas frames for review.
- Verifies that source canvases and Phaser frames retain authored dimensions after the resolve.
- Binds visual and performance evidence to standard JUnit reports without allowing a failed Playwright command to claim passage.

## Flight certification

- Passes both TypeScript compilers, architecture checks, and the complete isolated unit suite.
- Passes all 12 desktop/mobile visual scenarios with all-tier edge evidence and no clipped halo or dark alpha fringe.
- Passes four applicable desktop/mobile performance scenarios with six intentional project skips.
- Passes the unchanged 5 ms normal update-cost gate at a maximum observed p95 of 0.7 ms under local SwiftShader.
