# Release 1.7.0 — Tune the Starbound Helm

The helm now answers every pilot without redrawing the stars around them. Veterans can invite a harsher crossing, new captains can claim more room to recover, and the campaign's formations, warning lights, and battle rhythm hold their authored course.

## Three measures of the same frontier

- Adds persistent Low, Normal, and High difficulty choices to the shared settings deck.
- Names the campaign's original balance Normal and preserves it exactly with a `1.0` hull-damage multiplier.
- Uses `0.75` accepted hull damage on Low and `1.25` on High, changing forgiveness without changing encounter grammar.
- Leaves formations, spawn schedules, movement, projectile speed, telegraph windows, hazard geometry, boss phase thresholds, and the one-hit shield contract untouched.
- Applies a changed difficulty to the next accepted hull hit, including when the choice is made while gameplay is paused.

## One helm, wherever the pilot stands

- Unifies difficulty, visual quality, creativity, energy, ambience, and music volume behind shared controls instead of parallel menu implementations.
- Makes the full settings deck available from both the main menu and pause menu.
- Separates pause-time Checkpoints and Settings into explicit views while preserving resume, save, load, delete, and main-menu navigation.
- Keeps physics and gameplay audio paused while settings change.
- Persists pause-time visual quality for the next restart without reloading or discarding the active run.

## A deck that turns with the ship

- Fits the shared controls across desktop, phone portrait, and phone landscape compositions.
- Recreates slider clusters when responsive width changes, retaining current audio values while preventing stale hit areas, overlap, or clipping after rotation.
- Uses compact slider presentation where space is tight without hiding any setting.
- Returns each newly opened pause overlay to the Checkpoints view for a predictable flight-deck rhythm.

## Saves that remember the true wound

- Preserves finite fractional hull values through runtime normalization, checkpoints, and manual save/load.
- Keeps Low's `4.25` and High's `3.75` post-hit hull values exact instead of rounding them into unintended extra damage.
- Retains existing bounds for health and existing integer normalization for score, shields, lives, upgrades, and helper resources.
- Falls back safely to Normal when difficulty storage is missing, malformed, unsupported, or inaccessible.

## Quality corridor

- Adds focused coverage for difficulty persistence, centralized damage scaling, one-hit shields, fractional save round trips, shared settings ownership, pause-state invariants, and responsive slider lifecycle.
- Exercises main and pause settings on desktop and mobile, including landscape-to-portrait relayout without leaving the active run.
- Proves in Chromium that choosing Low while paused persists the setting, resumes normally, and changes the next accepted hull hit from `5.0` to `4.25`.
- Passes TypeScript 7 and TypeScript 6 compatibility checks, lint, unit tests, level validation, Knip, production build, bundle budgets, desktop/mobile functional and visual browser gates, and isolated performance evidence.
