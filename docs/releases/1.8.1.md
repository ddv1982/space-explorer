# Release 1.8.1 - Cool the Engines, Keep the Neon

The command deck still burns against the dark, but its reactor no longer spends every reserve on atmosphere. This patch trims invisible weight from the flight lanes so fanless ships can hold a steadier course without surrendering the neon-vector remake.

## Two skies for the long patrol

- Standard quality now flies with two independently scrolling background planes instead of three, cutting a full-screen layer of recurring overdraw.
- High remains the unabridged spectacle tier with its third additive atmosphere plane.
- Background generation now builds and retains only the authoring canvases and runtime planes required by the selected tier.
- A center-lane shade is baked into the far composite once, preserving Lane-Reading without adding work to the frame loop.

## Disciplined particle wings

- Adds `particleQuantityScale` to the existing Low / Standard / High quality contract.
- Low launches 60% and Standard 80% of authored burst quantities; High keeps the full formations.
- Standard particle textures fall from 2x to 1x resolution and burst scale tightens to 90%, reducing texture pressure and additive fill while impacts remain legible.
- Single-particle trails remain single particles, preserving projectile direction and hostile-lane readability.

## Sentries that inspect the light

- Gameplay visual evidence now samples luminance from real browser screenshots instead of merely confirming that a ship exists.
- Desktop evidence guards the brighter-edge, darker-corridor composition; portrait evidence guards the bounded dark-field contrast appropriate to its cropped background geometry.
- Game-over and victory evidence now checks title placement, viewport containment, and separation from command-deck eyebrow copy.

## Flight certification

- Passes TypeScript, lint, the complete isolated unit suite, production build, and desktop/mobile visual browser gates.
- Keeps encounter grammar, damage, movement, projectile speed, telegraph clocks, boss phases, and Arcade bodies unchanged.
