# Release 1.7.1 - Balance the Silent Command Deck

When the guns fall quiet, the bridge should give every command room to breathe. The pause deck now gathers its checkpoint stations around the pilot's eye, names each choice plainly, and keeps every control at its post as the viewport turns or narrows.

## One deck around the pilot

- Centers a wider checkpoint stack instead of leaving half the bridge empty for music controls that moved to Settings.
- Reduces the oversized pause title, widens the Checkpoints and Settings tabs, and gives each view guidance that matches the task at hand.
- Renames the checkpoint heading for a clearer relationship between the selected view and its three slots.
- Keeps the established neon flight-deck language while strengthening alignment, spacing, and scan order.

## Every command names its destination

- Removes the duplicate global Save Game and Load Game controls from the footer.
- Leaves save, load, and delete actions on their explicit checkpoint rows, so no command silently chooses a slot for the pilot.
- Reserves the global footer for Resume and Main Menu, the two actions that truly apply to the whole paused run.
- Preserves occupied, empty, disabled, and destructive slot states without changing checkpoint storage behavior.

## Stations that hold through every turn

- Keeps title, guidance, tabs, checkpoint labels, cards, status, and footer actions in distinct responsive bands.
- Fits desktop, phone portrait, phone landscape, short, and ultra-compact frames without clipping or overlap.
- Compresses nonessential copy and card metadata only where the viewport cannot safely carry the full presentation.
- Keeps the Settings deck readable through the same rotations and compact layouts.

## Quality corridor

- Adds rendered pause evidence for Checkpoints and Settings at `984x768` desktop and `390x844` phone portrait sizes.
- Adds geometry assertions for centered desktop cards and non-overlapping tabs, headers, rows, status text, and footer actions.
- Exercises pause, resume, resize, active-run settings, and tab switching in desktop and mobile Chromium.
- Passes TypeScript 7 and TypeScript 6 compatibility checks, lint, the complete unit suite, production build, and focused desktop/mobile browser gates.
