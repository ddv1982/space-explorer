# Release 1.10.1 - Focus the Glass-Tide Lens

Mira Shoal no longer arrives through a fogged command lens. The refit bay now gives every instrument its own berth, while high-density displays etch arrival telemetry with a cleaner, steadier hand.

## Sharper arrival instruments

- Supersamples canvas-backed intermission text up to `2x` on desktop Retina displays.
- Caps compact-device text resolution at `1.5x`, preserving the performance headroom reclaimed in `v1.10.0`.
- Snaps text to whole logical coordinates to avoid fractional-position softness.
- Raises desktop refit detail copy from 11 to 12 pixels for stronger small-type readability.

## Clear berths for every signal

- Gives each upgrade card a fixed status column so descriptive copy cannot cross into caps, prices, or lock states.
- Removes the duplicated progression-cap label that caused the Fire Rate readout to print through itself.
- Crops unusually long or localized detail copy at the status boundary as a final collision guard.

## Flight certification

- Adds deterministic coverage for desktop, compact, standard-density, and high-density text-resolution policy.
- Passes the complete unit suite, TypeScript checks, ESLint, production build, bundle gates, and whitespace validation.
