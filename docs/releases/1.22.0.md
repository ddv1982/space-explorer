# Release 1.22.0: Floaty flight, honest hazards, and a new command deck

Playtest feedback on 1.21.0: the controls were an improvement but still too precise, some lasers in later levels could be dodged by flying underneath them, and the menus needed polish.

## Flying

- Handling matches v1.19 again: 800 px/s² thrust, 400 px/s² drag, and an 800 px/s cap. The ship builds speed over about a second and slides roughly 800 px from full speed after you let go. Reversing takes real effort.
- Diagonals stay normalized, and the rendered bank frames and frame-rate independence from 1.21.0 stay.
- Touch drag now brakes early, so the ship trails your finger and eases onto it instead of overshooting.

## Hazards

- **Solar flare:** a full-height wall of fire with one gap, telegraphed for 1.3 s on screen before it sweeps across. Flying under it is no longer possible.
- **Laser lattice:** a curtain of beams from edge to edge with one gap. The telegraph scales with the distance to the gap (about 1.8 s on a phone, 2.9 s on a 1280 px desktop). Beams hurt only across their bright core, not their faint glow.
- **Asteroids:** ambient rocks and bursts reach the screen edges, rock-corridor walls run all the way to the edge, ring crossfire rocks angle inward and cross where you fly, wormholes open across 10–90% of the width, and asteroid hitboxes are round instead of square.
- Every flare and lattice layout is checked by a simulated pilot with a 250 ms reaction time, flying the real flight model on portrait, landscape and desktop screens.

## Menus

- The command deck shows the lit player ship idling over a launch pad (static under reduced motion), above the NEW RUN tile, the three save slots, and a SETTINGS button.
- Difficulty, visual quality and the four audio sliders open in a settings sheet. Escape or DONE closes it.
- The victory panel fits 390 px phones, and game-over and victory text no longer overlaps the frame ornaments.

## Verification

Both TypeScript compilers, lint, formatting, unit tests, level validation, unused-code and architecture checks, build and bundle limits pass locally. The flight, beam, menu, smoke and accessibility browser checks pass on desktop and touch projects. An independent Sol review requested three fixes (flare telegraph visibility and two pool sizes) and approved the result.
